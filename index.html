Letters from my name · optional

Use up to 40 characters. Keep the spelling you use, or leave empty to remove the name. Changes apply with Ready to grow. The name stays in this browser and is included in exported backups. Learning Garden does not upload names for narration or speak entered names.

Find a number, Count with me, and How many do you see are ready to play. Recognizing a numeral means finding a written number, such as 3. Counting a quantity means counting objects to find how many there are. This range applies to number practice, independently of your letter choices.

Device voices vary by device and installed voices. Automatic uses a local English voice when suitable, otherwise the original recordings. Names you enter are never spoken. Both buttons below preview this selection; press Ready to grow to save it for play.

See what we have explored

This is participation on this browser/device, not mastery or a score for a named child. A valid answer attempt, counting tap/help, or picnic letter tap records the activity and its target once in the summary. Simply opening an activity does not. Repeated taps and retries do not add duplicate letters or numbers. Discoveries count successful flower-earning rounds, including retries and help; adult help is not detected. Shared devices combine everyone's play. Earlier saves have no history to reconstruct; tracking starts with this update or the last clear. No dates, names, individual attempts, or telemetry are added to history.

Clear this device's practice summary? Flowers, collectibles, garden arrangement, mission, name and settings stay saved. Export a backup first if you want to restore this history later.

Back up or move your garden

Backups include all saved progress and settings, and may contain a child's entered name. Creating or importing a backup stays on this device; Learning Garden does not upload it to a service. You choose where to save or share the file.

On iPhone, use Share backup file → Save to Files when available, or Download backup. To move gardens, open this game on the other device and choose the saved file below.
